# Third-party notices

The themes `warm-mahogany`, `warm-gruvbox` and `warm-sand` (in `themes/`), their status bar palettes (in `extensions/warm-ui/palettes/curated.json`) and their terminal schemes are adapted from the `mahogany`, `dark-gruvbox` and `light-sand` themes of [oh-my-pi](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi). The status bar layout follows the oh-my-pi status line. The adaptation renames the themes, removes color keys that pi does not accept, and raises colors to the contrast standard in the README.
